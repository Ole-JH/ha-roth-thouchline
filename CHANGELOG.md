## v0.1.0

First release.

- Set up from the Home Assistant UI with your Roth Touchline SL app login.
- One thermostat per room, with heating or cooling shown as it happens.
- Temperature, humidity, battery and signal strength sensors per room.
- A new target temperature stays on screen while the Roth cloud catches up.
- Logs in again by itself when the session expires, and asks for your password if it changes.
- Rooms that are switched off stay visible as off instead of disappearing.
- Custom icon and logo (shown on Home Assistant 2026.3 and newer).
